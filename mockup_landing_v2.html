About

What is the FRA Remote Sensing Survey?

The Global Forest Resources Assessment Remote Sensing Survey provides independent estimates of forest area and change at national and regional levels using satellite imagery interpretation.

Trained interpreters classify land use at over 325,000 sample locations worldwide, following a rigorous protocol that allows comparison across countries and over time.

Results feed directly into FAO's flagship forest monitoring reports and inform international policy on deforestation, forest degradation, and sustainable land management.

2000
Baseline period Global land use status established as the foundation reference point.
2010
First change period Forest cover and land use changes between 2000 and 2010 quantified.
2018
Second change period Continued monitoring. Connects with FRA 2020 published results.
2024
Current period — FRA 2025 Most recent imagery interpreted. Results being validated and published.
